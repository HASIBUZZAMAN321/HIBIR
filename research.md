# Research

My research focuses on integrating artificial intelligence, biomedical imaging, computational modeling, wearable technologies, and advanced biomaterials to develop clinically translatable healthcare solutions. My work combines engineering innovation with medical applications to improve disease diagnosis, patient monitoring, and personalized treatment.

## Computational Hemodynamics

My research applies computational fluid dynamics (CFD) and fluid–structure interaction (FSI) to investigate cardiovascular biomechanics and patient-specific blood flow dynamics.

**Research Areas**

- Blood Flow Simulation
- CFD
- FSI
- Wall Shear Stress
- Pulsatile Flow
- Carotid Artery Disease
- Hemodynamic Analysis
