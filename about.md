## Biography

I am a biomedical engineer and researcher with interdisciplinary expertise spanning artificial intelligence, biomedical imaging, computational modeling, wearable healthcare technologies, and medical device development. My research integrates engineering principles with clinical applications to develop intelligent healthcare technologies that improve disease diagnosis, monitoring, and treatment.

Currently, I am a Project Researcher at the National Cancer Center (NCC), Republic of Korea, where I conduct research on 4D ultrasound imaging, computational fluid dynamics (CFD), cardiovascular biomechanics, patient-specific blood flow simulation, experimental phantom fabrication, and AI-assisted medical imaging.

Previously, I worked as a Biomedical Research Engineer at the National University of Singapore (NUS), developing wearable flexible biosensors, smart biomaterials, drug delivery platforms, and AI-enabled healthcare technologies. During my Master's degree at the Gwangju Institute of Science and Technology (GIST), my research focused on biomedical signal processing and deep learning for neurodegenerative disease classification.

My long-term research vision is to develop clinically translatable intelligent biomedical technologies by combining artificial intelligence, physics-based modeling, medical imaging, and advanced biomaterials.
