# Hasib's Integrated Intelligence in Biomedical Research (HIIBR)

Welcome to the research portfolio of **HASIBUZZAMAN MD**.

My research integrates biomedical engineering, artificial intelligence, computational modeling, medical imaging, biomedical signal processing, ultrasound imaging, wearable sensors, computational biomechanics, and digital health to develop innovative technologies for healthcare diagnosis, monitoring, and treatment.
