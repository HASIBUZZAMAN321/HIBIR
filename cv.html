Professional Profile

Biomedical engineer and researcher with interdisciplinary experience in artificial intelligence, biomedical signal processing, medical imaging, computational fluid dynamics, fluid–structure interaction, ultrasound imaging, wearable electronics, biomedical materials, and cardiovascular modeling.

My current research at the National Cancer Center focuses on physics-informed 4D ultrasound, vascular hemodynamics, computational fluid dynamics, fluid–structure interaction, vascular phantom development, and artificial intelligence for quantitative vascular assessment.

My previous research includes voice-based amyotrophic lateral sclerosis classification, deep learning for medical image analysis, wearable flexible sensing systems, electrospun functional materials, and computational optimization of left ventricular assist devices.
